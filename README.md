### Who I am

I'm a Software Engineering student based in Istanbul. I like software that has to behave correctly under pressure — not just run, but hold up when things go wrong.

That instinct is pulling me toward **cybersecurity**. I'm early in that path and I'm not pretending otherwise — no certifications, no CTF wins yet. What I do have is a builder's habit: I'd rather understand *why* something breaks than memorize that it does.

Until that shift is complete, I keep building. The project below is the most serious thing I've shipped so far, and it taught me more about how systems actually fail than any course did.

---

### What I've built

**Soundline** — on-chain, verifiable performance records for trading bots and AI agents.
Built on Sui, using Seal for commit-then-reveal signal encryption and Pyth for independent, tamper-proof result scoring. Smart contracts written in Move, live on testnet.

> The core idea: a bot can't quietly hide its losing trades if the result is sealed on-chain *before* the trade closes, and scored by a price feed it doesn't control.

Right now I'm mapping out the cybersecurity field itself — where the real depth is, and which corner (offensive, defensive, applied/secure-dev) actually fits how I think. No fixed lane yet, and I'd rather say that honestly than fake a specialization I don't have.
